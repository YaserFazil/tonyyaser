## 🎨 Design Mockup & Visual Strategy

### Design Philosophy:
- **Modern & Professional**: Clean, minimalist design that conveys expertise
- **Trust-Building**: Use of data visualization, metrics, and social proof
- **Conversion-Focused**: Clear CTAs, strategic placement, visual hierarchy
- **Real Estate Context**: Subtle real estate imagery (properties, blueprints, data dashboards)
- **Tech-Forward**: Modern UI elements suggesting innovation and automation

### Color Palette:
- **Primary**: Deep Blue (#1E3A5F) - Trust, professionalism
- **Secondary**: Teal/Cyan (#00BCD4) - Innovation, technology
- **Accent**: Orange (#FF6B35) - Energy, action, CTAs
- **Neutral**: Light Gray (#F5F7FA), Dark Gray (#2C3E50)
- **Success/Positive**: Green (#4CAF50) - Results, growth

### Typography:
- **Headings**: Bold, modern sans-serif (Inter, Poppins, or similar)
- **Body**: Clean, readable sans-serif (same family, regular weight)
- **Hierarchy**: Clear size differentiation (H1: 48-64px, H2: 32-40px, Body: 16-18px)

### Visual Elements:
- **Icons**: Modern, line-style icons for services/features
- **Illustrations**: Custom or stock illustrations showing automation concepts
- **Screenshots**: Real dashboard/workflow screenshots (if available)
- **Animations**: Subtle hover effects, scroll animations, micro-interactions
- **Images**: Professional real estate imagery, data visualizations, team/productivity concepts

### Layout Principles:
- **Whitespace**: Generous spacing for clarity
- **Grid System**: Consistent 12-column grid
- **Mobile-First**: Responsive design, mobile-optimized
- **Loading Speed**: Optimized images, lazy loading
- **Accessibility**: WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
